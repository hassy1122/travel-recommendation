About us

Advice from a well-travelled friend

Wayfarer exists because the big travel sites stopped being useful: too many pages, too little depth, and rankings nobody trusts.

Our position

Narrow and deep beats wide and thin

We publish eighteen destinations instead of four hundred. Each one gets a real budget breakdown, five to ten curated things to do, a month-by-month crowd and weather chart, and the honest “why go” that a directory page will never give you.

We expand only when the format is proven — because a hundred thin pages help nobody, and Google agrees.

Personalisation firstThe quiz scores destinations on your interests, budget, dates and style — and shows every reason.
Written on the groundEvery guide comes from someone who has been there, eaten there and paid for it themselves.
No paid rankingsDestinations are never ranked by payment. Sponsorships, if they ever exist, sit outside organic results.
Transparent moneyWe earn affiliate commissions on some booking links. The price you pay does not change.
The standard

What every destination page must contain

💷

Real budget

Split into budget, mid-range and luxury daily costs, updated at least twice a year.

📅

Seasonality

Twelve months of temperature, rainfall and crowd levels — not a one-line “best time”.

✓

Curated things to do

Five to ten picks with a cost note or a booking tip attached to each one.

💡

Local tips

The things you only learn on the ground — the ferry to take, the month to avoid, the market to skip.
