Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026. Written in plain English — the short version is at the top.

The short version: we store your wishlist, itineraries and preferences locally in your browser. Newsletter emails are used only for the newsletter. We do not sell personal data. Analytics cookies only load if you accept them.

1. Data controller

Wayfarer (“we”) operates this site. For privacy questions contact privacy@wayfarer.example.

2. What we collect

Stored in your browser only: saved destinations (wishlist), itineraries, packing lists, review drafts, quiz answers and cookie preferences. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly copy a share link.

Provided by you: your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter, and your name, email and message when you contact us or leave a review.

Collected automatically (only with consent): aggregated analytics such as pages viewed, referrer and approximate country, used to decide which guides are worth writing next.

3. Cookies

Essential cookies (or their localStorage equivalent) remember your wishlist and consent choice. Optional analytics cookies are blocked until you click “Accept all” in the banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent — analytics cookies and newsletter sign-up.
  • Legitimate interest — keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.
  • Contract — responding to messages you send us.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR and CCPA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data; object to processing; and request portability. Email privacy@wayfarer.example and we will act within 30 days. Because wishlist and itinerary data lives in your browser, you can erase it instantly via your browser’s site-data controls.

6. Processors & transfers

We use a reputable email provider for the newsletter and a privacy-respecting analytics provider. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data with third parties. Where data is processed outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Children

Wayfarer is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We will note material changes at the top of this page. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
